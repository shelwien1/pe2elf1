tsvcompG was this tree before the pass recorded in TSVCOMP-MODEL.md sections
7 and 8; tsvcompH is it after.  H is 11,347,066 bytes against G's 11,372,019,
and it holds 1020 MB of model tables where G held 928.  Every file is smaller
than balrog13, from 0.93% on 01 at the thinnest to 3.44% on 07, and 1.79%
over the corpus.

Decoding H takes 0.95x the time G did on 05.ogg and 03.ogg, 1.04x on 07.ogg,
1.01x on 09.ogg and 0.97x on 08.ogg -- unchanged, within the noise, in a
third of the address space.

tsvcompH is also what oggcomp produces: `oggcomp c` on the .ogg codes byte
for byte what `tsvcomp c` coded from `balrogg c`'s record stream, minus the
container's 7-byte header, which is the same length in both.  The sizes
above are oggcomp's.

oggcomp2 is oggcomp with rc.inc's coder in place of sh_v2f.inc's -- the
container's version 2 -- over the same model: 249 bytes more across the
seventeen files, 0.002%, for a coder that renormalises by bytes and does no
division, which is 6 to 11% off the wall time.  OGGCOMP-PLAN.md section 12.
